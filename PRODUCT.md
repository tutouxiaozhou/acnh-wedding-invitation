# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user: Vite + React + TypeScript, built as a mobile-first static invitation that remains presentable on desktop.

## Users

- Primary: wedding guests opening a private invitation link on their phones, commonly inside WeChat.
- Owner: a couple who wants an unmistakably Animal Crossing: New Horizons fan-made invitation prototype.

## Product Purpose

Create a recognizable Animal Crossing-themed wedding invitation that makes essential date, venue, schedule, and RSVP information clear while feeling like an authentic island-arrival journey.

## Positioning

The invitation borrows the recognizable rituals of New Horizons—Dodo Airlines arrival, island broadcast, NookPhone, Nook Miles, passport stamps, dialogue bubbles, and Wedding Season furniture—instead of merely using a generic cozy-island palette.

## Operating Context

- Shared privately as a link and experienced primarily in portrait orientation.
- Read in short sessions, often without sound.
- All wedding facts are demonstration placeholders until replaced by the owner.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Couple names, date, venue, schedule, directions, RSVP interaction, and closing message are required.
- The experience must work without audio and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- High-recognition game imagery is intentionally allowed for this private, non-commercial fan prototype.
- The page must state that it is unofficial fan work and must not imply Nintendo affiliation.
- Extracted assets may not be repackaged as a commercial template or asset library.

## Brand Commitments

- Binding user direction: recognition must be strong enough that a guest identifies Animal Crossing immediately.
- Use New Horizons-specific visual language and selected recognizable character/item imagery.
- Tone remains warm, celebratory, playful, and sincere rather than parody-like.

## Evidence on Hand

- The previous original island-life version was explicitly rejected as insufficiently recognizable.
- The user delegated asset selection and composition.
- No final names, date, venue, photographs, or real RSVP backend were provided; demo facts must be labeled.

## Product Principles

- Recognition is a first-viewport requirement, not a footer disclaimer.
- Use game rituals as information architecture, not scattered decorative stickers.
- Keep logistics obvious even when the visual world is playful.
- Make every fan asset serve a specific invitation moment.
- Preserve an explicit private, unofficial, non-commercial boundary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard-visible focus, semantic controls, adequate contrast, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion support are required.
- Audio remains optional and non-essential.
